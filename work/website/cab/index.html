Website design · Insurance brokerage

Turning insurance options into clearer customer journeys.

Caribbean Assurance Brokers serves customers across multiple insurance needs. The design challenge was to keep the experience broad enough for the business, but simple enough that a visitor can quickly understand which path is relevant and what to do next.

01 · About the company

A growing brokerage that needs to make choice feel manageable.

Caribbean Assurance Brokers operates in Jamaica and connects customers with insurance options across different needs. As the business grew, the website needed to support a wider service story without turning navigation into a long list of products.

The goal was to make the first few seconds useful: help visitors identify the right category, understand the brokerage value and move toward assistance with confidence.

100+ employees

The company’s growth created a stronger need for a professional, scalable digital presence.

Multiple insurance categories

The experience must reduce choice overload without oversimplifying the offering.

Broker-led service

The site should make human help feel easy to access, not buried behind generic forms.

02 · UX & design approach

Organize around the customer’s decision, then support the broker conversation.

01

Define customer tasks

Reframed navigation around common insurance needs rather than internal service labels.

02

Use decision-friendly summaries

Introduced short, comparable descriptions before deeper policy information.

03

Keep help visible

Placed contact and broker-support cues near moments where uncertainty is likely to rise.

04

Design for expansion

Used repeatable product and service patterns that can support future categories without changing the core structure.

04 · Outcome & impact

A more organized path from insurance need to human help.

Less overload

Visitors can understand the service landscape without reading every product page first.

Better handoff

Broker contact is positioned as part of the journey rather than an afterthought.

Scalable

Reusable page patterns support a growing brokerage and wider service offering.

Reflection

Sometimes the best UX move is simply helping people choose where to start.

Insurance comparison can get complicated very quickly. A good brokerage site does not try to turn every visitor into an expert—it helps them frame the problem well enough to move forward with the right support.
