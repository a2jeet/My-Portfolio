01 — About the company

Insurance choice across many needs and customer types.

Caribbean Assurance Brokers (CAB) is a Jamaican multi-line insurance brokerage offering international insurance, employee benefits, credit union plans, general insurance and individual insurance services. The company began operations in 2005 and has grown from 11 employees to more than 100.

That breadth created a UX challenge: customers should not need to understand the company’s internal divisions before they can find the insurance route that fits their need.

02 — UX & design approach

Organize around customer journeys, not internal departments.

The design approach emphasized task clarity. Product families were translated into understandable customer routes, while high-value actions such as getting a quote, resuming an application and renewing a policy were treated as primary interaction points. Visual design reinforced confidence and accessibility rather than dense corporate presentation.

04 — Outcome & impact

A more customer-oriented structure for a complex brokerage portfolio.

The resulting design approach prioritizes customer intent over organizational complexity, supporting faster access to products and service actions. Where verified analytics are unavailable, the impact story remains grounded in the scale and operational context the interface had to support.
