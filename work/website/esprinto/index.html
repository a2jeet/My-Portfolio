01 — About the company

An electric mobility brand balancing product choice and rapid growth.

e-Sprinto is an Indian electric two-wheeler brand focused on sustainable, safe and stylish mobility. Its website presents multiple scooter models and also supports business growth through dealer acquisition, finance relationships, battery-swapping partnerships and after-sales communication.

The design challenge was to make the website feel energetic and product-forward without losing practical information such as model comparison, business partnership opportunities and ownership support.

02 — UX & design approach

Lead with the product. Build confidence around ownership.

This project took a more visual, consumer-oriented approach than the enterprise and insurance websites. The experience was shaped around high-impact scooter imagery, model-led discovery and strong lifestyle storytelling, supported by clear paths to dealer enquiries and ownership information. The visual system had to feel youthful and energetic while keeping technical content readable.

A stronger product-first experience with room for brand and network growth.
