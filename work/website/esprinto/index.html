Website design · Electric mobility

Giving an electric mobility brand a sharper, more product-led digital presence.

e-Sprinto needed a website that could make its scooter range feel exciting and easy to explore while still supporting the practical questions around ownership, dealership opportunities and brand confidence.

01 · About the company

An electric two-wheeler brand competing for attention in a fast-moving market.

e-Sprinto is an Indian electric mobility brand focused on practical, accessible two-wheelers. Unlike an enterprise website, this experience needed to work harder emotionally: visitors should be able to feel the product, compare choices and imagine ownership before they move into specifications or enquiries.

The challenge was to create energy without creating noise—and to make dealer and ownership journeys feel like part of the same brand story.

Product first

Vehicle discovery and model differentiation form the strongest entry point into the brand.

Consumer confidence

Specifications, support and ownership information need to reinforce the visual story.

Business growth

Dealer and partnership pathways have to sit naturally alongside customer journeys.

02 · UX & design approach

Lead with desire, then give people the confidence to go deeper.

01

Make models easy to scan

Used a clear product hierarchy so visitors can understand the range before opening individual specifications.

02

Create visual momentum

Balanced bold product imagery and large typography with enough whitespace to keep the site feeling premium rather than promotional.

03

Layer the detail

Kept essential product information visible early while reserving technical depth for model pages and supporting sections.

04

Connect growth journeys

Designed dealership, enquiry and ownership-support paths as deliberate extensions of the main product experience.

04 · Outcome & impact

A stronger digital showcase that leaves room for both product and network growth.

More visual

The product range has room to lead the story instead of being buried in text-heavy sections.

Easier to explore

A clearer model hierarchy helps visitors move from discovery to product detail with less effort.

Built to grow

The same system can support future vehicles, campaigns, dealer programs and ownership content.

Impact is kept qualitative because verified conversion or post-launch analytics were not supplied.

Reflection

Consumer product websites need emotion and utility in the same frame.

The project reinforced something I value in visual design: a bold first impression only works when the next step is equally clear. The product should create interest, but the structure has to turn that interest into understanding.
