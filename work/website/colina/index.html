01 — About the company

A long-standing financial brand serving everyday life decisions.

Colina is a diversified Bahamian financial services and insurance business covering life, health, auto, home, marine, mortgages and investments. The company describes a heritage of more than 155 years and serves customers across both protection and financial planning needs.

The website needed to feel reassuring and established, while still helping customers quickly find the right product, request a quote, make a payment or access service information.

02 — UX & design approach

Design around confidence and the next useful action.

Unlike a technology services website, this experience needed to reduce uncertainty. The design approach emphasized product discovery, clear category language, trust cues and prominent service actions. Rather than introducing wireframe-heavy process documentation, the work focused on mapping customer intent to content, refining the visual hierarchy and creating polished responsive page patterns.

04 — Outcome & impact

A clearer digital front door for a broad insurance portfolio.

The design direction created a simpler route from awareness to action and helped unify multiple insurance and financial product lines under one coherent visual experience. No unverified conversion uplift is claimed; instead, the story demonstrates the breadth of content and the customer actions the interface was designed to support.
