01 · Context

A specialist Salesforce business needed an identity of its own.

Damco’s established Salesforce practice was moved to Achieva, creating an opportunity to present the capability as a dedicated Salesforce brand rather than one service within a broader technology portfolio.

Achieva brings together Salesforce developers, administrators, consultants and architects across consulting, implementation, engineering, integrations, AppExchange, managed services and AI-led Salesforce solutions. The challenge was to reposition that depth without making existing customers feel they had entered an unfamiliar business.

Salesforce Summit Partner

A specialist positioning supported by a mature Salesforce delivery practice.

100+ Salesforce projects

The new experience needed to communicate established capability—not the uncertainty of a new startup.

Multi-cloud expertise

Sales, Service, Marketing, Experience, Financial Services and other Salesforce capabilities needed a scalable information structure.

02 · The challenge

Change the brand without breaking the business continuity behind it.

01

Create a new identity

Define a new name, visual language and digital personality that could become recognisable independently of the parent brand.

02

Preserve familiarity

Keep the experience credible and recognisably Salesforce-focused so existing customers could understand the transition immediately.

03

Reframe the offering

Turn a broad set of Salesforce capabilities into an understandable service architecture for buyers with different needs and maturity levels.

04

Protect acquisition

Plan the migration around continuity of search intent, lead paths and conversion journeys rather than treating launch as a purely visual exercise.

03 · Design brief

Fresh, specialist and Salesforce-native—without looking derivative.

01

Brand positioning

Frame Achieva as an experienced Salesforce specialist with the confidence of an established practice and the energy of a focused new brand.

02

Visual direction

Build a cleaner, contemporary system with strong hierarchy, approachable enterprise styling and visual cues that feel at home in the Salesforce ecosystem.

03

Content hierarchy

Lead with business outcomes, then expose services, cloud expertise, industry context, proof and accelerators progressively as users explore.

04

Conversion continuity

Keep consultation and enquiry actions visible throughout the experience so the rebrand does not interrupt established lead behaviour.

04 · Process

From an inherited practice to a coherent standalone experience.

01

Audit & retain

Reviewed the existing Salesforce service footprint, audience needs and valuable content paths to identify what should survive the transition.

02

Define the system

Established the new brand personality, hierarchy, reusable UI patterns and rules for presenting Salesforce services consistently.

03

Restructure journeys

Organised services around consulting, AI, engineering, integrations, AppExchange, managed services and implementation, with supporting industry and solution pathways.

04

Prototype & validate

Designed key page types responsively and reviewed navigation, content density, CTA visibility and continuity across service journeys before scale-up.

07 · Outcome

A new brand with continuity built into the transition.

Brand distinction

The Salesforce practice gained a focused identity and digital presence designed to stand independently while retaining the credibility of an established team.

Customer continuity

The transition was structured to retain existing relationships and familiar enquiry paths while opening the brand to new customer acquisition.

Lead & search continuity

Initial disruption to SEO and lead flow was kept limited, followed by growth as the new brand and content footprint became established.

The outcomes above reflect the supplied project context. I have intentionally kept them qualitative rather than inventing acquisition, conversion or SEO percentages that were not formally provided.

08 · Reflection

A rebrand succeeds when the audience experiences progress, not disruption.

The most important design decision was treating Achieva as more than a visual refresh. The identity, service architecture, UX and migration logic had to work together. A new name can create attention, but continuity in content, trust signals and conversion paths is what helps an established practice carry its equity forward.

The result is a more focused platform for communicating Salesforce expertise while leaving room for the offering to expand into AI, accelerators, industry solutions and new Salesforce capabilities.
