01 — About the company

A large services story needed a simpler digital structure.

Damco Solutions is a global technology partner serving businesses across software engineering, cloud, data, AI, automation, insurance technology, design and other enterprise service areas. The company highlights experience across 25+ industries, 700+ projects and a global delivery model.

The website therefore had to communicate breadth without becoming overwhelming, while giving decision-makers fast routes into services, industries, capabilities and proof.

02 — UX & design approach

Content architecture first. Visual hierarchy second.

For a large B2B site, the main design challenge was not decoration—it was helping users understand where to go. The approach focused on organizing the service catalogue into clear families, creating repeatable page patterns, prioritizing proof points and CTAs, and building a visual system that could scale across many service and industry pages without looking repetitive.

04 — Outcome & impact

A system designed to support scale, not just a single homepage.

The redesign approach created a more structured way to present a large enterprise portfolio, making it easier to expand the site as new offerings and industry content were added. In the absence of verified conversion analytics, the portfolio story focuses on the measurable content scale and the design system’s ability to support it.
