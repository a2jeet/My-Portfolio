UI/UX design · Enterprise modernization · Animal health

Modernizing a legacy operational system without losing the workflows people depend on.

A cross-platform Microsoft UWP application for Animal Health International, designed to replace an aging legacy experience with clearer navigation, easier onboarding and a scalable interface ready for a broader Microsoft ecosystem.

ClientAnimal Health International
RoleUI/UX design

01 · Context

A mature animal-health business was being held back by an aging operational interface.

Animal Health International is part of Patterson Companies and serves producers, veterinarians and retailers with animal-health products, technology, expertise and support. Its long operating history meant the underlying business workflows were valuable—but the legacy application used to support them had become increasingly difficult to evolve.

The design challenge was not to reinvent those workflows for novelty. It was to preserve the operational knowledge embedded in the system while making the experience easier for new employees to learn and simpler for the business to maintain and extend.

Legacy technology

An aging application stack constrained interface improvements and future development.

Steep learning curve

New employees had to understand a dense, dated interaction model before they could work confidently.

Limited scalability

Adding features and connecting newer capabilities was becoming harder and more expensive.

02 · Problem framing

The real problem was bigger than an outdated interface.

01

Usability debt

Dense screens, inherited navigation and inconsistent interaction patterns increased cognitive load.

02

Talent scarcity

Specialist knowledge around the older technology stack made support and enhancement harder to sustain.

03

Maintenance cost

Routine changes required more effort than they should, increasing the cost of keeping the system useful.

04

Feature friction

The legacy structure made it difficult to introduce new capabilities without adding further complexity.

03 · Research & discovery

Understand what users need to preserve before deciding what to change.

The discovery model for a legacy modernization project focuses on task observation, stakeholder conversations, existing-screen audits and workflow mapping. Rather than treating every legacy behavior as a requirement, the goal is to separate essential business logic from interface habits created by technical limitations.

Primary persona

Operations employee

Needs: fast access to frequent tasks, predictable navigation and clear system feedback.

Pain point: too much legacy knowledge is required to complete routine work confidently.

Secondary persona

New employee

Needs: recognizable labels, guided task flow and an interface that can be learned progressively.

Pain point: unfamiliar screens and hidden conventions make onboarding slower.

Business persona

System administrator

Needs: maintainable patterns, scalable modules and room for integrations.

Pain point: small changes in the legacy product can create disproportionate maintenance effort.

Research insight

Frequent tasks should be easier to reach than the full system hierarchy suggests.

Research insight

Modernization must reduce dependency on memorized paths and specialist tribal knowledge.

Research insight

Consistency and scalability matter as much as visual modernization.

04 · Information architecture

Simplify the system around tasks, products and operational context.

05 · UX design process

Move from workflow evidence to a reusable cross-platform system.

01

Audit

Inventory legacy screens, repeated patterns, high-frequency tasks, terminology and known usability issues.

02

Map

Translate existing workflows into task flows and identify steps caused by business rules versus legacy constraints.

03

Simplify

Restructure navigation, reduce unnecessary decisions and establish consistent interaction patterns.

04

Prototype & validate

Prototype representative journeys, review them with stakeholders and refine the reusable UI system before development.

07 · Solution

A modern UWP application designed for clarity today and extension tomorrow.

Cross-platform foundation

Simplified navigation

Task-oriented information architecture and product navigation reduce the effort required to find and complete routine work.

Scalable UI

09 · Outcome

A stronger operational foundation with a lower learning burden.

Robust & scalable

The modernized structure provides a stronger foundation for future capabilities and integrations.

Easier to use

Clearer navigation, consistent patterns and task-led flows make the system more approachable for both experienced and new employees.

Lower maintenance

A modern technology and UI foundation reduces dependence on aging patterns and makes ongoing enhancement more manageable.

Reflection

Legacy modernization is as much about preserving knowledge as replacing technology.

The strongest modernization decisions came from understanding why a workflow existed before changing how it looked. By separating essential business logic from legacy interaction debt, the new experience could become simpler without becoming unfamiliar—and more scalable without forcing users to relearn their work from scratch.
