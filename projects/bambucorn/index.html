01 — Context

Crowdfunding needs confidence on both sides of the platform.

Bambucorn is a crowdfunding platform licensed by the Central Bank of Bahrain (CBB), enabling investors to participate in equity and debt opportunities they believe in.

The platform supports ambitious entrepreneurs and property owners seeking to raise capital from $100,000, while investors can participate from $1,000 and diversify exposure to early-stage ventures.

02 — Design challenge

Make a regulated process feel clear, credible and achievable.

01 / ONBOARDING

Reduce enrollment friction

Turn a multi-step signup journey into a clear progression so new users always understand what is required next.

02 / VERIFICATION

Integrate prescribed checks

Accommodate third-party, government-prescribed identity verification without making the experience feel disconnected from the core product.

03 / ACCESS

Design for multiple roles

Control product access and available actions according to the user’s role, verification state and responsibilities.

03 — My role & scope

From website structure to a testable end-to-end product experience.

This was my first complete project designed in Figma in 2021. I created the website experience, mapped the user journeys, designed the onboarding and verification screens, defined role-dependent access states and connected the complete experience into an interactive prototype.

The prototype gave stakeholders a realistic way to test forms and transitions before implementation, discuss third-party API integration in context and validate how different user roles moved through the platform.

04 — Roles & permissions

One platform. Different goals. Controlled access.

Rather than exposing the same navigation and functionality to everyone, the experience was structured around the intent and permission level of each user type.

ROLE 01Investor

Complete verification, discover investment opportunities, review relevant information and progress toward investing.

ROLE 02Entrepreneur

Create a profile, provide required information and prepare a fundraising campaign for an eligible venture.

ROLE 03Property Owner

Progress through the appropriate onboarding and fundraising journey for property-led opportunities.

05 — Onboarding & verification

Keep compliance in the flow—not in the way.

The onboarding journey was designed to make identity and eligibility checks feel like part of one continuous experience, even when prescribed verification depended on an external service.

01Create account and capture essential profile information.
02Identify the user role and reveal relevant requirements.
03Hand off to the prescribed identity / verification process.
04Return verified users to role-appropriate platform access.

09 — Outcome

A clearer route from interest to verified access.

Faster enrollmentA structured multi-step journey helps users understand and complete onboarding with less friction.
Easier verificationThird-party checks are positioned as part of one coherent experience rather than an isolated interruption.
Controlled accessRole-based permissions help expose the right functionality to the right users at the right stage.

Reflection: As my first Figma project, Bambucorn was also an important shift from designing isolated screens to designing connected product behavior. Mapping permissions, external verification and realistic form interactions into one prototype made the stakeholder review far more useful and established a stronger end-to-end UX workflow for future projects.
